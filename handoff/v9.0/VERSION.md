# Rival v9.0 design boundary

- Package: `v9.0`
- Status: prospective scratch-policy architecture; not yet active training authority
- Design branch: `rival-v9-scratch-design`
- Branch base snapshot: `0642742c17e5abc8476d3487185b264e2446cb68`
- Date: 2026-08-23

## Intent

Freeze the action and observation contracts for a from-scratch Rival before another large training campaign is authorized.

## Relationship to M08

Milestone 08 remains an independently valuable experiment and its evidence/checkpoints must be preserved. v9 does not rewrite M08 history. It replaces the prospective policy architecture after M08 reaches a coherent final boundary.

## Non-negotiable v9 architectural choices

1. New Rival actor is trained from scratch; Wisp is not embedded in its policy graph.
2. Native action cadence is one Rocket League physics tick: 120 policy/controller updates per simulated second.
3. No finite lookup table is the policy action ceiling.
4. Actor can express all five RLBot analog controller axes continuously and all eight combinations of jump/boost/handbrake.
5. `RivalObsV1` is generated by one canonical feature implementation shared by RocketSim training and RLBot deployment.
6. Serious training is forbidden until train/deploy observation and one-tick action-timing parity gates pass.
7. Wisp/Nexto are opponents, benchmarks, and optional curriculum anchors only.
